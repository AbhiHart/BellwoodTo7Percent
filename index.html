Q2 · Growth and footprint

The neighbor-metro clinic doesn't pay. Drag the slider and see why.

A $12–18M clinic adds $3–5M a year of fixed cost. At the case's own economics it needs more extra patients than both of Bellwood's ortho clinics see today. We fund a virtual front door and visiting surgeons instead, and win back the 31,500 referrals a year already lost at home.

No new sites, no closures, no service-line exits. The suburban ortho clinic becomes the musculoskeletal hub using its idle 30% of slots; urgent cases keep protected core slots.

Month-12 option

Consolidate 2 subscale clinics into hubs, only if 90% of affected patients gain no more than 15 minutes of travel and keep their clinicians. Worth $1.7–4M a year.

Q3 · AI and automation

Fund what is proven. Pay for the rest on results. Refuse two uses outright.

Each use is placed by its value inside 18 months and the strength of the evidence behind it. Bubble size is its cost. About $3.3M of AI spend lands inside the window.

Placement is the team's judgment from the case pilot and benchmarks, not a measured score.

Guardrails on every model

AI never lowers a patient's urgency. A nurse reviews a 5% weekly sample of "routine" referrals. Go-live needs 98% sensitivity with no subgroup gap above 3 points. Every model has a kill switch.

AI scheduling, $5.0M

Half the fee is paid on outcomes. Rolled out by clinic group from month 13; if group 1 hasn't lifted booking by 2 points by month 16, the second tranche is cancelled.

Never traded for savings

Six safeguards sit above every number on this page.

Binding stop rule

Same-day pause for serious harm, statistical stops by site, and an equity stop if any access gap widens by more than 20%. The lever owner can't resume it.

Staffing floors

Set by the nurse staffing committee. Agency requests auto-approve after 4 hours. Agency never falls below its FY23 level.

No layoffs while vacancies exist

Hiring freezes only per access wave. WARN and union steps for every wave and every consolidation.

No perverse incentives

Coding and documentation dollars are excluded from executive pay. The staffing vendor is never paid on total spend.

AI never lowers urgency

Nurse review of routine referrals, sensitivity and subgroup gates, outreach-only no-show prediction, a kill switch per model.

Compliance by design

Stark and Anti-Kickback structures for every physician arrangement, EMTALA screening before any redirect, 60-day overpayment refunds.
